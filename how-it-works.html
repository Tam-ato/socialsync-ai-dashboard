THE SOCIALSYNC METHOD

Four steps. One clearer direction.

STEP 01

Understand your business.

Share your business type, audience, location, goals and the kind of content you can realistically produce.

STEP 02

Choose the right channels.

Review a shortlist of social platforms that may suit your audience, objectives and resources. You decide which ones to prioritise.

STEP 03

Find your content angle.

Turn your idea into platform-specific directions: photo shot lists, carousel storyboards, video concepts, professional posts or local updates.

STEP 04

Plan your next move.

Use a campaign outline to organise the message, format, audience and call to action before you publish.

BUILT AROUND REALISTIC RESOURCES

Good content doesn't have to mean a big production team.

SocialSync considers the creative resources you say you have. A business with phone photography can start with useful images and clear carousels. A team producing video can explore short-form video and tutorials. A professional service can start with practical insights and case studies.

Recommendations are starting points, not promises of reach, sales or engagement. Actual results depend on the audience, execution, platform and market.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A few things worth knowing.

Do I need every social platform?

No. Start with the platforms that best fit your audience and goals. It is often more practical to maintain a small number of channels well.

Does this version use AI?

The current demo uses business-profile rules and reusable campaign templates. It does not yet call a live AI model or publish content to social accounts.

Can I use the ideas commercially?

Use the outlines as starting points, check factual claims and permissions, and adapt the content to your business voice before publishing.
